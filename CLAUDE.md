## Explaining things to the engineer

- Before naming a tool, file, setting or new label (e.g. pnpm-workspace.yaml, allowBuilds, "only when searched"), say in plain words what problem it solves, what it is, and where the engineer will see it or what triggers it.
- Give one decision per bullet. Do not pack several jargon-heavy choices into one line.
- End reports with the exact steps the engineer should take to test the change and what they should see.

- When reporting status, first answer the engineer's actual question in plain words (e.g. "Yes, all our changes are on GitHub"), then give details like commit hashes.
- When calling something "new" or "changed", say what it is new or changed compared to.
